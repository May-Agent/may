Watch a command's output or a file's appended lines and receive new lines as they arrive, without polling. `source` is either `{"command": "<source>", "interpreter": "shell"|"python"}`, which runs the source inside the sandbox, or `{"file": "<path>"}`, which follows a file across rotation and truncation. Only non-empty increments are delivered; the end of the watch is delivered too. `timeout_secs` is required and at most {{ max_timeout_secs }}: at the deadline the process tree is killed and the end is reported as a timeout.
