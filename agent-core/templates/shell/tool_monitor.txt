Watch a command's output, an existing background job, or a file's appended lines and receive new lines as they arrive, without polling. `source` is `{"command": "<source>", "interpreter": "shell"|"python"}` (runs inside the sandbox), `{"pid": <pid>}` (attaches to a running background job and stops when it exits), or `{"file": "<path>"}` (follows a file across rotation and truncation; requires `stop_on` since files have no natural end). Prefer `{"pid": <pid>}` over `{"file": ...}` when watching a background command. Only non-empty increments are delivered; the end of the watch is delivered too. `timeout_secs` is required and at most {{ max_timeout_secs }}: at the deadline the process tree is killed and the end is reported as a timeout.
